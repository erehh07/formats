Understanding Cinema Aspect Ratios

What is an Aspect Ratio?

Aspect ratio is the width to height relationship of the movie frame. A wider ratio looks more cinematic and epic. A taller ratio fills more of your vision. Directors choose different ratios depending on the kind of story they want to tell. For example: 1.85:1 means the frame is 1.85 times wider than it is tall.
