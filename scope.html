SCOPE

2.39 : 1

Scope is an ultra-wide anamorphic format. It is the widest frame in mainstream cinema. The image is almost two and a half times wider than it is tall. It gives films a vast, epic feeling, perfect for action, westerns, sci-fi, and historical epics.
